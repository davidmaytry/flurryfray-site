Two teams, two campfires,
one field of fresh snow

Keep your fire burning and put out theirs. The snow is the battlefield, and it keeps falling: dig trenches, pile up walls, plough through drifts. Cozy, silly, physical –
not a survival game. No hunger, no crafting, no grind: just snow, friends and a campfire worth fighting for

Snow in the face,
nobody gets hurt

Scroll

What you do in the snow

Scoop, pack, throw

Every hit knocks the warmth out of a kid – or the heat out of a campfire. Freeze, and you pop back at your own fire. Nobody gets hurt; everybody gets snow in the face

Guard your campfire

Bury the rival fire under a snowdrift and keep your own one roaring. The team whose fire is still burning wins

Build a snowman together

Roll giant snowballs and stack them with a teammate. A finished snowman pays out toys, garlands and a star

Decorate the tree

Hang the toys on your team's Christmas tree. Finish decorating it first and you win – campfire or not

Thaw out the loot

Gear lies frozen in ice all over the field. Carry it to your fire and thaw out a flamethrower, a snowblower, a shovel or a fuel canister

Couch co-op first

Splitscreen on one TV with gamepads, or two players on one keyboard. Online by IP or LAN, or solo against bots
